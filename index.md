I'm a first year student in the Research Master in Economics at Tilburg University. I'm a passionate advocate for women and children's rights, and I'm interested in the intersection of microeconometrics and gender-based inequality in the labour market.

Before starting this master's programme, I obtained my bachelor degree in Econometrics and Operations Research at Tilburg University. In addition, I have 3 years of experience in Romania's first youth-led activism association, Girl Up Romania, and 2 years of experience in the UNICEF Student Team Tilburg. I've participated in a research project at the National Bank of Romania, and completed the CentER Honours Research Experience at Tilburg University. 
</div>

My goal is to obtain a PhD in Econometrics, and the research areas that interest me are:

* **Gender-Based Inequality:** Investigating structural disparities in the labour market, wage gaps, and the motherhood penalty.
* **Economics of Crime:** Understanding the socio-economic determinants of crime, evaluating targeted policy interventions and investigating potential biases in justice bodies.
* **Health Economics:** Examining regional disparities in healthcare, the impact of environmental stressors on fertility outcomes and healthcare utilization patterns among women or vulnerable groups.
